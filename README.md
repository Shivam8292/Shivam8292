I am a **Software Developer** focused on building intelligent systems, semantic search engines, and AI-integrated full stack products. I specialize in designing and deploying high-performance **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** pipelines, building robust backend APIs, and crafting intuitive user interfaces.

- 🎓 **Education:** Pursuing B.E. (Hons.) in Computer Science Engineering (AI & ML) at Chandigarh University (CGPA: 7.88/10)
- 🏢 **Experience:** Former AI Developer Intern at **EOXS**, where I engineered AI-powered ERP automation workflows.
- ⚡ **Focus:** Practical AI workflow automation, vector databases (ChromaDB), semantic similarity search, and backend optimization (FastAPI, Asyncio).
- 🧩 **Fun Fact:** I enjoy breaking down complex algorithms and indexing everything from codebases to resumes.
